Miyan Content Publisher

A personal tool to publish my own short videos to TikTok.

What it does

  • Lets me sign in with my own TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit.
  • Uploads and publishes short videos I create to my own TikTok account using the Content Posting API.
  • Reads basic profile info (display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected.

Who it is for

This app is operated by Miyan Andi Irawan for managing his own content. It is not a public service and does not collect data from other users.
