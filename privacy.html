Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Miyan Content Publisher ("the App"), operated by Miyan Andi Irawan, handles information when you connect a TikTok account.

1. Information we access

With your permission via TikTok Login Kit, the App may access:

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): open ID, display name, and avatar — used only to show which account is connected.
  • Video upload and publish (video.upload, video.publish): used only to send videos you choose to your own TikTok account.
  • Video list (video.list), if granted: used only to show the status of videos you posted.

2. How we use it

Information is used solely to provide the App's publishing function. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely by the integration service used to run the App and are used only to make requests on your behalf. Videos are transmitted directly to TikTok and are not retained longer than needed to complete the upload.

4. Third-party services

The App relies on TikTok's APIs and an integration platform to manage authentication. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies, including the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time from your TikTok account settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services). You may also email us to request deletion of any stored data.

6. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
